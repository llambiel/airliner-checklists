Boeing 707 checklist

4 × JT3D turbofan · three-crew with a flight engineer · EPR thrust

Every phase for the Boeing 707, as flown in a flight simulator — panel scans marked as flows, read-and-respond cards marked as checklists. Open the interactive version to tick lines off, fill in your speeds and keep your place between sessions.

  • -120 — The first production build, short body. JT3C turbojets on the earliest airframes.
  • -320B — The intercontinental one with the extended wing and JT3D fans. The definitive 707.
  • -320C — Convertible passenger and freight, with a main deck cargo door.
  • E-3 / KC-135 family — Military relatives on the same airframe. Related, but their systems and these items diverge quickly.

Flight Engineer Panel Flow · from memory

  1. ELECTRICALGENERATORS OFF — BUS TIES SET
  2. FUEL PANELTANKS 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 + RESERVES — PUMPS ON

    3. Four mains, two reserves and a centre tank on most builds, all balanced by hand. The engineer flies the fuel while the pilots fly the aeroplane.

  3. HYDRAULICSUTILITY & BOOST CHECKED
  4. PNEUMATICSBLEEDS SET
  5. PRESSURIZATIONFLT & LAND ALT SET
  6. AIR CONDITIONINGPACKS AS REQUIRED
  7. ANTI-ICEOFF
  8. EPR / TAKEOFF DATACOMPUTED — BUGS SET

    9. Thrust is set on EPR, worked out from the charts by the engineer. There is no computer to check the arithmetic.
