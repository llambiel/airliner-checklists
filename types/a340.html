Every phase for the Airbus A340-300, as flown in a flight simulator — panel scans marked as flows, read-and-respond cards marked as checklists. Open the interactive version to tick lines off, fill in your speeds and keep your place between sessions.

  • -200 — Short body, long range. Same CFM56-5C flows as the -300.
  • -300 — The common CFM56 A340 and the basis for these checklists.
  • -500 / -600 — Different aeroplane underneath — Trent 500s and a four-wheel centre bogie. Engine and fuel items differ from what is listed here.
