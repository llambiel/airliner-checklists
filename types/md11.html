3 × turbofan · No. 2 is the tail engine · slats/flaps in degrees

Every phase for the McDonnell Douglas MD-11, as flown in a flight simulator — panel scans marked as flows, read-and-respond cards marked as checklists. Open the interactive version to tick lines off, fill in your speeds and keep your place between sessions.

  • MD-11 — Passenger. Two-crew flight deck — the flight engineer's work is automated away.
  • MD-11F — Freighter, and how most surviving MD-11s finished their careers. Main deck door, wide CG range.
  • MD-11 Combi — Passengers forward, freight aft. Loading discipline matters — check the trim setting against the load sheet.
