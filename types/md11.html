Preflight Flow · from memory

  1. BATTERY SWITCHON
  2. EXTERNAL POWER SWITCHESAS REQUIRED
  3. ENGINE / APU FIRE TESTPERFORM

    4. All 3 engine fire handles and the APU handle illuminate, the 3 engine fuel shutoff switches illuminate, both master warnings, fire bell, level 3 alerts on the EAD.

  4. MASTER WARNINGPRESS
  5. FUEL USEDRESET
  6. ANNUNCIATOR LIGHTSTEST / CHECK
  7. ANNUNCIATOR BRIGHT / DIM SWITCHSELECT
  8. CARGO FIREMANUAL TEST

    9. FWD / AFT heat and smoke lights, manual test switch, all agent discharge lights, FWD / AFT flow switch DISAG lights, both master warnings, CRG FIRE LWR FWD + LWR AFT on the EAD.

  9. IRU SWITCHESNAV

    10. Full align needs the aircraft stationary; enter present position if prompted.

  10. IRU ALIGNMENTINITIALIZE
  11. COCKPIT VOICE RECORDERTEST
  12. GALLEY BUS PANELALL LIGHTS EXTINGUISHED
  13. CARGO TEMPERATURE SELECTORSAS REQUIRED
  14. FADEC / ENGINE IGNITION PANEL LIGHTSOFF EXCEPT ENG IGN OFF
  15. HYDRAULIC TESTPERFORM — NORMAL ON SD
  16. ELECTRICAL PANELCHECK

    17. Manual light off, smoke elec/air source normal, drive 1/2/3 and CAB BUS guarded, emergency power selector armed with no light.

  17. AIR PANELVERIFY

    18. Manual, ECON and TRIM AIR OFF lights off, cabin outflow valve open, air conditioning established, temperature selectors as required.

  18. FUEL PANEL MANUAL LIGHTOFF
  19. FUEL QUANTITYTEST

    20. 188880 total and 10500 in each tank displayed during the test.

  20. EMERGENCY LIGHT SWITCHARM
  21. NO SMOKING SWITCHON
  22. SEAT BELT SWITCHOFF
  23. EXTERIOR LIGHTSAS REQUIRED
  24. EVAC PANELARMED AND GUARDED
  25. GPWS TEST SWITCHTEST AND GUARDED
  26. AFS PANELCHECK

    27. All lights off, flap limit normal, elevator feel normal.

  27. CABIN PRESSURIZATION PANELCHECK

    28. In AUTO, valve open, ditching switch guarded, ditching light extinguished.

  28. ANTI-ICE PANELCHECK — ALL LIGHTS OFF
  29. WINDSHIELD ANTI-ICE / DEFOGNORM / ON — DEFOG LIGHT OUT
  30. ALTIMETERSET QNH
  31. IAS / HDG / FEET / BANK SELECTORAUTO
  32. STATIC AIR SWITCHNORMAL
  33. SOURCE INPUT SELECTOR LIGHTSOFF
  34. PFD / ND / EAD / SDCHECK

    35. No faults, altimeters as desired, time correct, oil quantity above 16 quarts, PFD FMA showing TAKEOFF.

  35. GEAR HANDLEDOWN — 4 GREEN
  36. AUTO BRAKE SWITCHRTO
  37. OVERBOOST BREAKOUT BARFULL AFT
  38. THROTTLESCHECK — CLOSED

    39. Check travel and the aural warnings, then close them.

  39. REVERSE LEVERSDOWN
  40. DIAL-A-FLAPTO SETTING
  41. ENGINE START BUTTONSPRESSED IN
  42. FUEL SWITCHESOFF
  43. SDCPCUE LIGHTS AND CLEAR

  46. TRANSPONDERSET

Engine Start Flow · from memory

  1. ENGINE 3 START SWITCHPULL

    2. Order 3 – 1 – 2, tail engine last.

  2. FUEL LEVERON

    3. Once N2 is at or above 15%.

  3. OIL PRESSUREVERIFY RISING
  4. ENGINE IGNITIONVERIFY ≤ 25 SECONDS
  5. EGTCHECK
  6. START VALVEVERIFY CLOSED AT 45% N2
  7. ENGINE 3VERIFY STABILIZED

    8. N1 around 20%, EGT around 400 °C, N2 around 60%, oil within 2 quarts of the pre-start figure, fuel flow around 1200 at sea level.

  8. ENGINE ANTI-ICEAS REQUIRED
  9. ENGINE 1REPEAT
  10. ENGINE 2REPEAT
