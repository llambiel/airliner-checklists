Boeing 737 MAX checklist

2 × CFM LEAP-1B · two-crew · four large displays

Every phase for the Boeing 737 MAX, as flown in a flight simulator — panel scans marked as flows, read-and-respond cards marked as checklists. Open the interactive version to tick lines off, fill in your speeds and keep your place between sessions.

  • MAX 8 — Ships with most simulators as the stock 737. Typical takeoff flaps 5.
  • MAX 9 — Stretched — same procedures, longer body, tail strike margin reduced.
  • MAX 10 — Longest of the family with a semi-levered main gear that extends the strut on rotation.
