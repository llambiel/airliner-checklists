Embraer E-Jets checklist

2 × GE CF34 · two-crew · Primus Epic five-display flight deck · flaps 1–5 / FULL

Every phase for the Embraer E-Jets, as flown in a flight simulator — panel scans marked as flows, read-and-respond cards marked as checklists. Open the interactive version to tick lines off, fill in your speeds and keep your place between sessions.

  • E170 — The baseline, CF34-8E. Around 70 seats and the shortest of the family.
  • E175 — The regional workhorse in North America — same CF34-8E, slightly stretched.
  • E190 — Bigger wing and CF34-10E engines. Enough aeroplane to sit against a 737 on thin routes.
  • E195 — The longest of the first-generation family. Same flows, more seats, less range.

Engine Start Flow · from memory

  1. PUSHBACK / START CLEARANCEOBTAINED
  2. START/STOP SELECTOR 2START
  3. FUELINTRODUCED AT 20% N2

    4. The FADEC runs the start and will abort it by itself on a hot or hung start. Monitor ITT and N2 and keep your hand off the selector.
