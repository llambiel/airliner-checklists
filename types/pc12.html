Pilatus PC-12 checklist

1 × PT6 turboprop · single-pilot · flaps 15 / 30 / 40

Every phase for the Pilatus PC-12, as flown in a flight simulator — panel scans marked as flows, read-and-respond cards marked as checklists. Open the interactive version to tick lines off, fill in your speeds and keep your place between sessions.

  • PC-12/45 — The early one. Analogue panel, separate power and condition levers, and a start you fly yourself.
  • PC-12 NG — PT6A-67P behind a Honeywell Apex glass deck. Still a manual start, still a condition lever.
  • PC-12 NGX — PT6E-67XP with dual-channel FADEC. One power lever, an automated start, and the prop scheduled for you.

Preflight Scan Flow · from memory

  1. POWER LEVERIDLE
  2. CONDITION LEVERPC-12/45 / PC-12 NGFUEL CUT OFF

    3. Gone on the NGX — the PT6E takes one lever and works the propeller out for itself.

  3. FUEL PUMPS / TANK SELECTAUTO

    4. The system alternates tanks on its own. A manual selection left running is how most PC-12 imbalances start.

  4. INERTIAL SEPARATORAS REQUIRED

    5. Out for unpaved strips and for visible moisture near freezing. It costs a little torque and buys the compressor a quiet life.

  5. ANTI-ICEBOOTS · PROP · WINDSHIELD CHECKED
  6. OXYGENCHECKED — MASK IN REACH

    7. Single pilot in the low thirties. Know where the mask is without looking for it.

  7. AVIONICSFLIGHT PLAN · PERF · WEATHER LOADED
  8. WEIGHT & CGWITHIN LIMITS
  9. TAKEOFF DATATORQUE · V-SPEEDS · TRIM SET

Engine Start Flow · from memory

  1. IGNITIONAUTO
  2. STARTERENGAGED — Ng RISING
  3. FUELPC-12/45 / PC-12 NGINTRODUCED AT 13% Ng

    4. Yours to judge on the /45 and the NG. Watch ITT through light-up and cut the fuel yourself if it runs at the limit.

  4. STARTPC-12 NGXAUTOMATIC — MONITORED

    5. The NGX starts on one button. The FADEC schedules fuel and ignition and aborts a hot start without asking — keep your hand near the switch anyway.

  5. ITTMONITORED — WITHIN LIMITS

    6. The transient limits are a few seconds long, not a target to aim at.

  6. STARTEROFF — IDLE STABLE
  7. GENERATORON — CHARGING
  8. OIL PRESSURE / TEMPRISING — IN GREEN
  9. EXTERNAL POWERDISCONNECTED
