Airbus A400M Atlas checklist

4 × TP400-D6 · counter-rotating props · tactical airlifter

Every phase for the Airbus A400M Atlas, as flown in a flight simulator — panel scans marked as flows, read-and-respond cards marked as checklists. Open the interactive version to tick lines off, fill in your speeds and keep your place between sessions.

  • Logistic — Point to point with a paved runway at each end. Flown much like any other Airbus with a cargo hold.
  • Tactical — Short unpaved strips, low level, and a landing you plant rather than smooth. Different speeds, different mindset.
  • Airdrop — Ramp and door open in flight. CG, slowdown speeds and the drop configuration are briefed on the ground, not worked out en route.
  • Air-to-air refuelling — Pods or centreline hose. The tanker plan drives fuel, altitude blocks and the whole descent profile.
