Daher TBM 900 / 940 / 960 checklist

1 × PT6A turboprop · single-pilot · 330 kt on one engine

Every phase for the Daher TBM 900 / 940 / 960, as flown in a flight simulator — panel scans marked as flows, read-and-respond cards marked as checklists. Open the interactive version to tick lines off, fill in your speeds and keep your place between sessions.

  • TBM 900 — The first of the winglet-and-single-lever generation. Garmin G1000 and a lot of speed for the fuel.
  • TBM 930 — Same airframe with the G3000 touchscreen deck. Menus instead of knobs — know where the pages live before you need them.
  • TBM 940 — Adds autothrottle and automatic de-icing. The throttle does the engine limits so you can watch the aeroplane.
  • TBM 960 — PT6E-66XT with dual-channel FADEC and an electronic propeller. Start and power management are automated end to end.
