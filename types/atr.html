ATR 42 / 72 checklist

2 × PW127 turboprop · two-crew · torque and Np, not N1 · flaps 15 / 30

Every phase for the ATR 42 / 72, as flown in a flight simulator — panel scans marked as flows, read-and-respond cards marked as checklists. Open the interactive version to tick lines off, fill in your speeds and keep your place between sessions.

  • ATR 42-500 — The short one with six-blade props. Analogue-era panel with EFIS.
  • ATR 42-600 — Glass flight deck with five displays and a multi-purpose computer. Same airframe underneath.
  • ATR 72-500 — The stretch, and the one most operators fly. Heavier, and the wing works harder in ice.
  • ATR 72-600 — The current build — glass deck, same handling. What most simulator add-ons model.

Engine Start Flow · from memory

  1. AREACLEAR — PROPS CLEAR
  2. ENG 2 STARTPRESSED
  3. CONDITION LEVER 2AUTO AT 10% NH

    4. Watch ITT through the light-up. A hot start on a PW127 goes bad quickly, and the abort is yours to make, not the computer's.

  4. ENG 1START
  5. PROP BRAKERELEASED
  6. GENERATORSON
