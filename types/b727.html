Boeing 727 checklist

3 × JT8D tail-mounted · three-crew · EPR thrust · flaps 2/5/15/25/30/40

Every phase for the Boeing 727, as flown in a flight simulator — panel scans marked as flows, read-and-respond cards marked as checklists. Open the interactive version to tick lines off, fill in your speeds and keep your place between sessions.

  • -100 — The original short body. Lightest, and the one that could get into the small fields the type was designed for.
  • -200 — Stretched by 6 m. Watch the tail strike margin — the aft body sits close to the runway on rotation.
  • -200 Advanced — Higher weights, JT8D-17s and an APU as standard. Most surviving -200s are Advanced.
  • -200F — Freighter conversion. No cabin items; brief the loadmaster and check the trim against the load sheet.

    5. Tank 2 is the centre tank and feeds first. Nothing balances the mains for you — the engineer does it by hand with the crossfeeds.

  5. CROSSFEED VALVESCLOSED
  6. PRESSURIZATIONFLT & LAND ALT SET — RATE MID
  7. ANTI-ICEOFF
  8. AIR CONDITIONINGSET
  9. EPR / TAKEOFF DATACOMPUTED — BUGS SET

    10. JT8Ds are rated in EPR. The engineer works the numbers from the charts and sets the bugs; there is no FMC to do it.

Engine Start Flow · from memory

  1. IGNITIONLOW
  2. ENGINE 3 STARTENGAGED

    3. Order varies by operator — 3-1-2 with the centre engine last is common. Follow your add-on's checklist.

  3. START LEVER 3IDLE AT 20% N2
  4. ENGINE 1START
  5. ENGINE 2START

    6. No. 2 breathes through the S-duct in the tail, so its start looks and sounds different from the pods.
